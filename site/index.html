VTE Watch

Organizes NASA-referenced blood-clot (VTE) risk, scans, symptoms and medication checks over the mission for flight-surgeon review, with every safety check running onboard.

Synthetic data. A reference for clinician review, following NASA's published method — not a diagnosis, prediction or prescription. It does not detect clots.

Clinical scope: who uses it and what it does not do
  • Astronaut: fills in the daily symptom checklist; an emergency symptom gets the onboard emergency instruction at once, without waiting for the ground. Cannot change rules, clear an alert or approve medication.
  • Crew medical officer: enters ultrasound and lab results.
  • Flight surgeon: reviews the packet and signs the decision. Every medication and clinical decision is theirs.

It does not diagnose DVT, IJV thrombosis or cerebral venous sinus thrombosis; treat the NASA score as a validated probability; fill gaps between scans or read missing data as normal; rule out thrombosis when no symptoms are ticked; or start, dose, stop, hold or reverse anticoagulation. Built for crew in a mission medical surveillance programme only, not for patients on Earth or children. Approval level reached: synthetic-data demonstration only.
